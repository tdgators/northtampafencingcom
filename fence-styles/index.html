Fence Styles

The most common fence styles we build for homeowners across the Tampa Bay area.

Get a Free Estimate Call 813-669-4555

Privacy Fence

Solid, tightly-fitted panels — usually vinyl, wood, or composite — built to fully block sightlines into your yard. Our most requested residential style for backyards, pools, and properties that back up to neighbors or busy roads.

Learn More →

Semi-Privacy Fence

The same solid-panel look as a full privacy fence, but with small gaps (typically 1/2" to 4") between boards, so light and airflow get through while still screening most of the view. A popular middle ground for homeowners who want privacy without a fully closed-in feel.

Learn More →

Horizontal Fence

A modern, high-end look built from horizontal boards or slats instead of vertical pickets. We build horizontal fences in wood, composite, and vinyl for homeowners who want a contemporary style that stands out from the typical privacy fence.

Learn More →

Picket Fence

The classic front-yard fence — evenly spaced vertical pickets, usually 3'-4' tall, in wood, vinyl, or aluminum. Decorative rather than for privacy, and a great way to add curb appeal without blocking your yard from view.

Learn More →

Split Rail Fence

An open, rustic style built from horizontal wood rails set between posts, with no pickets at all. Common for marking large property lines, farm and ranch use, or a natural look along a driveway, and often paired with wire mesh if you need to actually contain pets or livestock.

Learn More →

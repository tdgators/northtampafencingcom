Planning your budget

Your Dream Fence, on a Budget That Works

A few decisions make the biggest difference in what your fence costs.

Material

Chain link and wood cost the least up front; vinyl and aluminum trade a higher price for less upkeep; composite and custom colors sit at the top.

Height & Length

Linear footage matters most. Taller privacy fencing costs more per foot than a four-foot pool or picket fence.

Gates & Details

Each gate adds to the total, as do decorative tops, custom colors, and removing an old fence.
